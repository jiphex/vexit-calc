UKRI 2025 Voluntary Exit Scheme Terms

  • Below pension age: 1 month per year of service, max 21 months
  • Above pension age: 1 month per year of service, max 6 months
  • Tapering: Maximum compensation = lesser of 21 months OR (months to pension age + 6)
  • Overall cap: £95,000 maximum payment
  • Applies to MRC, Research Council, UKAEA, and Civil Service Pension Scheme members
Note: This calculator provides an estimate based on typical Civil Service Voluntary Exit Scheme rules. Actual payments may vary based on your specific department's terms, pension scheme details, and individual circumstances. Please consult with HR for an official calculation.
